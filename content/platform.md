## Section: Why a platform (the connective-tissue strip)

*Short strip directly under the hero — three lines, no heading needed on the page. This is the thesis; everything below is proof.*

Point tools make you switch, re-upload, and re-explain. Lexli doesn't — because every tool here shares the same cases, files, and context.

Tomorrow's board updates itself because the platform already tracks your matters. Drafts arrive pre-filled because it knows the case type and the parties. eFiling packets assemble because your scanned pages are already OCR'd, merged, and indexed.

That's the difference between a bundle of tools and a workspace.

## Group 2 — The intelligence layer

*Transition line into the group:* One record is only useful if you can talk to it. This is the layer that reads, understands, and answers.

### Section: AI Assistant `#ai-assistant`

**Eyebrow:** THE ORCHESTRATOR

**Headline:** One assistant that can use every tool.

**Body:**
The Lexli Assistant is where daily work happens. Ask it about a case and it answers from that case's actual files. Ask it to draft, translate, or look something up, and it calls the right Lexli tool itself — you stay in one conversation.

**Feature bullets:**
- **Case-aware answers** — point it at a case and it works from the record: files, orders, hearing history.
- **Agentic tool calling** — it can run Drafting, the Translator, Case Finder, and the rest as tools inside your chat, and hand you the output.
- **Upload and ask** — drop in a scan or a PDF and question it directly.
- **Web search built in** — for live information beyond your files: judgments, statutes, current updates.

- **It remembers you** — user memory carries your preferences and patterns across conversations. `[VERIFY: shipped]`

### Section: Document Intelligence `#document-intelligence`

**Eyebrow:** THE FOUNDATION

**Headline:** Built to actually read legal documents.

**Body:**
Legal paperwork is messy — scanned annexures, stamped pages, tables inside affidavits, hundred-page agreements. Lexli's document intelligence is built to understand exactly this: it interprets text, tables, images and stamps, and long legal content from PDFs, forms, contracts, amendments, annexures, and executed agreements — and turns all of it into clean, structured data you can work with.

This is the quiet layer everything else stands on. The board updates itself because your matters are already understood. Drafts arrive pre-filled because the platform knows the case type and the parties. eFiling packets assemble because scanned pages are already OCR'd, merged, and indexed.

### Section: Legal Research `#legal-research`

**Eyebrow:** RESEARCH

**Headline:** Research that knows which case you're fighting.

**Body:**
Generic legal search gives you the law. Lexli gives you the law *for this matter* — because research here starts from your case record, not from a blank box.

**Feature bullets:**
- **Live legal web search** — judgments, case law, statutes (IPC, BNSS, and more), and government resolutions (GRs).
- **Case-aware research** — answers grounded in the matter's own files and history.
- **Arguments & rebuttals preparation** — build your side, and anticipate the other side's.
- **Hearing-by-hearing case notes** — a running note for every hearing, kept with the case.
- **IRAC summaries** — summarise cases and files in the structure you'd actually use.
- **The practical calculators** — limitation period, court fees, and jurisdiction lookup for a given case.
