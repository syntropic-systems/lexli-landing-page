## Why a platform (strip under the hero)

*Three short paragraphs, no heading on the page. This is the thesis; the rest of the page is proof.*

Point tools make you re-upload the same file and re-type the same party names. **The parties are already in the file. Lexli takes them from there.**

Tomorrow's board updates itself because the platform already tracks your cases. Drafts arrive pre-filled because it knows the case type and the parties. eFiling packets assemble because the scanned pages are already OCR'd, merged, and indexed.

That is the difference between a bundle of tools and a workspace.

## Group 2 — the intelligence layer

*Transition line:* A record is only useful if you can ask it questions. This is the layer that reads and answers.

### AI Assistant `#ai-assistant`

**Eyebrow:** The orchestrator

**Headline:** One assistant that can use every tool.

**Body:**
The AI Assistant is where daily work happens. Ask about a case and it answers from that case's actual files. Ask for a draft or a translation and it runs the right Lexli tool itself, inside the same conversation. **You ask once; it brings the tool to you.**

**Feature bullets:**
- **Case-aware answers** — point it at a case and it works from the record: files, orders, hearing history.
- **Runs the tools for you** — Drafting, Legal Translator, Case Finder, and the rest, called from inside your chat, output handed back to you.
- **Upload and ask** — drop in a scan or a PDF and question it directly.
- **Web search built in** — judgements, statutes, and current updates beyond your own files.
- **Voice mode** — speak instead of type. `[VERIFY: shipped]`
- **Remembers how you work** — your preferences and patterns carry across conversations. `[VERIFY: shipped]`

### Document intelligence `#document-intelligence`

**Eyebrow:** The foundation

**Headline:** Built to read legal documents as they actually arrive.

**Body:**
Legal files do not arrive clean. They arrive as scanned annexures, stamped pages, tables inside affidavits, and hundred-page executed agreements — with a stamp across the signature block. Lexli's document intelligence is built for exactly this. It reads text, tables, images and stamps, and long legal content from PDFs, forms, contracts, and amendments, and turns them into clean, structured data the software can actually use.

This is the layer everything else stands on. The board updates itself because your cases are already understood. Drafts arrive pre-filled because the platform knows the case type and the parties. eFiling packets assemble because the scanned pages are already OCR'd, merged, and indexed.

*(Prose only, no bullets — this is the moat argument, not a checklist.)*

### Legal Research `#legal-research`

**Eyebrow:** Research

**Headline:** Research that starts from the case, not from a blank box.

**Body:**
Generic legal search gives you the law. Lexli gives you the law for this case — because research here begins from the record: the files, the parties, the history.

**Feature bullets:**
- **Live legal search** — judgements, case law, statutes (IPC, BNSS, and more), and government resolutions (GRs).
- **Case-aware research** — answers grounded in the case's own files and history.

- **A note for every hearing** — running case notes, kept with the case.
- **IRAC summaries** — cases and files summarised in the structure you would use yourself.
- **The working calculations** — limitation period, court fees, and jurisdiction for a given case.

### Security `#security`

**Eyebrow:** Security

**Headline:** How your data is protected.

**Body:**
Lexli runs on audited infrastructure. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, network traffic is filtered before it reaches us, and production access is limited, logged, and reviewed.

**Detail bullets:**
- **Hosted on Azure** — inheriting Azure's physical security, environmental controls, redundant power, and compliance baseline.
- **Encryption** — AES-256 at rest; TLS 1.3 in transit across public networks.
- **Network protection** — Cloudflare WAF coverage, DDoS mitigation, and global CDN before traffic reaches us.
- **Access control** — production access is least-privilege, short-lived, fully logged, and reviewed.

**Compliance paragraph:**
Lexli is built to the controls that SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 define, and is GDPR-ready. Built to those controls — not yet audited against them. We state it that way because the distinction matters, and because you would catch it if we didn't.
