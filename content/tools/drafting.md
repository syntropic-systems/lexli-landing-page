## Better inside the platform

Drafting alone saves typing. Drafting inside Lexli saves the part before the typing: the platform has already read the case file, so the parties, the case type, and the court arrive in the draft without being asked for. The finished draft saves to the case, versioned — and if it is headed for a portal, eFiling Support takes it from there.

Nothing is filed until you have read it and said yes. **Lexli prepares. You decide.**
