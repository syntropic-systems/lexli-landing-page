## Features

- **Auto-run, every day** — the board schedules itself; you do not rebuild it each evening.
- **Multiple advocates, multiple courts** — one board for the whole office's appearances.
- **Verified against official cause lists** — the board reflects what the court published, and re-checks as lists change. `[VERIFY: shipped]`
- **Latest orders, attached** — each entry carries the case's most recent orders.
- **Opposition details** — the advocate on the other side, named on the entry.
- **Case timetable** — which case, where, and when, in one view.
- **Export with order files** — PDF or doc, including the last orders, ready to print or forward.
- **Available through the AI Assistant** — "what's my board for tomorrow" is a question, not a task.
