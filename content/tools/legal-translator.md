## How it works

1. **Upload or scan.** A PDF, a photo of a page, a scanned order — the document as you have it.
2. **Translate.** Between English, Hindi, and Marathi. Nine more Indian languages — Bengali, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese — are scheduled. `[COMING: keep the nine listed as scheduled, not available]`
3. **Review side by side.** The comparison view shows translation against original, with warnings where a term deserves your eye. `[VERIFY: what the warnings actually mark]`
4. **Edit and export.** Correct it in place, with version history. Export .doc or .pdf, or assign it to the case.
