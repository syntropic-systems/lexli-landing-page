## How it works

1. **Scan or upload.** Phone camera or laptop — certified copies, annexures, the page with the stamp across it.
2. **Digitise.** The scan becomes an editable file — .doc or .pdf — with the text recognised, not just photographed. `[VERIFY: OCR scope — one board note reads "OCR (only for eFilling)"; confirm whether OCR/searchability ships on every scan or only in the eFiling flow. 3. **File it.** Assign the output to a case, or download it. Recent scans and outputs stay in the upload modal, so the file you made yesterday is one tap away.
