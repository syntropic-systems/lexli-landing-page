## How it works

1. **Choose the case.** New or existing — the packet builds from the case file either way.
2. **The eDraft fills itself in.** Party names, case details, form fields — extracted from the case files, not re-typed into a portal form. `[VERIFY: extraction shipped]`
3. **The documents are made ready.** OCR conversion, merging, blank-page removal, and indexing, to the format the portal expects. `[VERIFY: per-portal format coverage — which portals]`
4. **You review. You approve. Then it files.** Every field and every page is yours to check before submission — and submission goes through your own portal credentials, not ours. `[COMING?: submission itself — confirm live vs preparation-only]`

## Features

- **eDraft creation from case files** — the form filled from the record, before you open it.
- **Filled-form editing and verification** — every extracted field is editable, and nothing is final until verified.
- **Document readiness** — OCR, merge, blank-page removal, indexing: the unglamorous requirements, met.
- **Your credentials, your filing** — submission happens through the user's own portal account. Lexli never files on its own authority.
- **New and existing cases** — first filing or the hundredth in a running case.
- **eDraft management** — save, revise, and export (.doc) drafts before submission.

## Where this sits in the platform

eFiling Support is where the rest of the platform's work converges. The scan you took in Document Scanner is already OCR'd. The draft you wrote in Drafting is already in the case file. The party details the eDraft needs were extracted when the case record was built. The packet assembles because the platform has been preparing it since the case arrived.

What never changes: nothing is submitted until you have read it and approved it.
